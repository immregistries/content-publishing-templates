The Executive Summary and Scope form the front section, above Chapter 1; they
have no section-bar entry and are not counted as chapters. Add topic sections
between `01` and `80`, numbered in reading order, and list each one here and in
the assembly file. Keep the document to six to seven
chapters, not counting appendices, and state each fact in only one section.
Appendices are numbered `80` and up and their headings begin "Appendix".
