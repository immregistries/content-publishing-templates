# Code of Conduct

This project brings together people from public health, clinical care,
standards development, and software implementation who may disagree about
{{TITLE}}. Good disagreement makes the work stronger.

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent;
- engage with ideas and evidence, not people;
- welcome questions from those new to the topic;
- keep discussion relevant to the project; and
- respect privacy, and never share personal health information or another
  person's private information.

Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that do not follow this
code, and may restrict participation by those who repeatedly disregard it. To
raise a concern privately, contact a repository maintainer through GitHub.
