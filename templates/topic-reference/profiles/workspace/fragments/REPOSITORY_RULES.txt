## Workspace

- This is a Workspace Topic Reference kept in a shared folder. Access follows
  the rules of that folder.
- Canonical source is the Markdown in `content/`. Notes, drafts, and documents
  in the folder are working material: list them in `sources/README.md` and
  write reviewed prose in `content/`.
- Do not copy material from this workspace to public places, including the
  shared templates under `process/`.
- Do not place credentials, personal health information, or contact lists in
  `content/`.
