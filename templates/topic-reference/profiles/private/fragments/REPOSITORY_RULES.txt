## Private repository

- This repository is private. Its InteropHub topic is private, and the Topic
  Reference is restricted to people who may see that topic, who may include
  AIRA staff and partner organizations; see `INTERNAL-USE.md`.
- Do not publish it. Do not enable GitHub Pages, add a publishing workflow,
  register a published address in InteropHub, make the repository public, or
  share built copies with anyone who cannot see the topic without explicit
  approval from the maintainers. GitHub Pages sites built from private repositories can be
  publicly readable.
- Write in `content/` only what every reader of the topic may see. When the
  topic's readers include partners, AIRA's internal plans, funding strategy,
  and information about one partner that others may not see belong in AIRA's
  own working material, not here.
- Never copy material from this repository to public places: the shared
  templates under `process/`, public repositories, public InteropHub topics,
  or issues and pull requests in public projects.
- Do not commit credentials, personal health information, contact lists,
  email, meeting recordings, or personal metadata in Office files.
- Source material in private working folders is not copied here. List it in
  `sources/README.md` and write reviewed prose in `content/`.
