# Contributing

{{TITLE}} is AIRA internal working material, kept in {{LOCATION}}; see
[INTERNAL-USE.md](INTERNAL-USE.md). Questions, objections, examples, and
proposed wording from AIRA staff and authorized collaborators are welcome.

## Ways to take part

- **Comment on the Topic Reference** in InteropHub. Sign in, open the hosted
  guide from [{{TOPIC_NAME}}, topic {{TOPIC}}]({{TOPIC_URL}}), and comment on
  any section. Comments stay in InteropHub, visible only to people who may see
  the topic.
- **Join the discussion** on the InteropHub topic.
- **Comment on a generated document.** Comments in a Word or HTML copy are
  review inputs; the Author transcribes them into `review/`.
- **Raise it in a meeting.** Meeting feedback is summarized in a dated file in
  `review/`.

Do not edit generated files in `output/` or `CoreDocument.md` as if they were
source; changes made there are lost at the next build. Have one person edit a
chapter at a time, since the workspace has no merge or branch history.

## How changes are decided

The Author considers feedback, discusses it where needed, writes the language
in `content/`, and records the outcome in `content/90-working-decisions.md`. A
working decision is the current position for discussion, and it can be
revisited.

Contributions become part of this Topic Reference's internal working material
and are covered by [INTERNAL-USE.md](INTERNAL-USE.md).
