Feedback arrives as signed-in comments on the Topic Reference hosted in
InteropHub, meeting discussion, and comments on generated Word or HTML
documents. None of it changes the Topic Reference until it is adjudicated
into `content/`.

- **Guide comments** stay in InteropHub with their dispositions. Record the
  resulting changes here as a readable change log, without commenter names.
- **InteropHub discussion notes and meeting feedback** are summarized in a
  dated file here, such as `2026-11-15-planning-call.md`. Record the substance
  and source, not personal details.
