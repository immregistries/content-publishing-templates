## Kept in a workspace, without a repository

- This Topic Reference is private. Its InteropHub topic is private, and the
  Topic Reference is restricted to people who may see that topic, who may
  include AIRA staff and partner organizations; see `INTERNAL-USE.md`.
- It is kept in a shared workspace and deliberately has no Git repository. Do
  not create one, push it to GitHub, or publish it anywhere except its
  topic's private hosting in InteropHub.
- Canonical source is the Markdown in `content/`. Notes, drafts, and documents
  in the working folders are working material: list them in
  `sources/README.md` and write reviewed prose in `content/`.
- Write in `content/` only what every reader of the topic may see. When the
  topic's readers include partners, AIRA's internal plans, funding strategy,
  and information about one partner that others may not see stay in the
  working folders, not here.
- Do not copy material from this workspace to public places, including the
  shared templates under `process/`.
- Do not place credentials, personal health information, contact lists, or
  local file paths in `content/`.
